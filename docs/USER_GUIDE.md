# User guide

## Players

### 1. Create an account
1. Click **Sign Up** (top right).
2. Fill in **Personal Information**: profile picture (required), full name, first and last name, gender, date of birth, district, weight (kg), height (cm).
3. Fill in **Contact & Identification**: NIC number, email, contact number, password (twice).
4. Under **Identity Verification**, pick the document type and upload the **front** and **back** images.
   - No NIC, passport or licence? Choose **School Letter** and upload a letter certified by your Principal or Vice Principal.
   - Accepted files: PNG or JPEG (large photos are shrunk automatically), or PDF under 2 MB.
5. Click **Create Account**. You are taken to the sign-in page. Your account is now **pending** until an admin verifies it.

### 2. Sign in
Use your **email** or **NIC number** and password. After 10 failed attempts in 15 minutes, sign-in is blocked for a while.

### 3. Register (pay the registration fee)
1. On your profile, after your account is **Verified**, click **Register Account**.
2. Enter the bank **Reference Number** and upload the payment slip (photo or PDF).
3. Click **Submit**. Once an admin approves it, your profile shows **Registered**, your **Registration Number** (SLBF ID) and the **Expiry Date** (one year later).
4. To renew, use **Register Account** again under *Extend your registration in SLBF* and submit the new payment slip.

### 4. Join or change club
In **Club Information**:
- **Select Open Club / Select Close Club** — first time joining. Search for the association, then the club, and upload the **offer letter**.
- **Request Open / Close Club Change** — moving clubs. Also enter the **resignation date** and upload the **resignation letter** from your current club.

Click **Submit Request**. When an admin approves it, your club and SLBF ID update automatically.

### 5. Edit your details
Click **Edit Profile**, change your first/last name, email, contact number, weight or height, then **Save Changes** → **Confirm & Save**. (Date of birth, NIC and district can only be corrected by SLBF.) Click **Cancel** to discard changes.

## Admins

Sign in with an admin account; you land on the **Admin Dashboard**. The cards open each queue:

| Card | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Pending Players' Verifications** | Click **View** on a player, check the ID images (click to enlarge; PDFs show page 1 with an **Open PDF** link), then **Approve** (assigns the SLBF ID) or **Reject** → **Yes, Reject** |
| **New & Renew Player Registration Requests** | **View** the payment slip and reference number → **Approve** sets the registration for one year from today |
| **Club Change Requests** | **View** the letters and requested club → **Approve** updates the player's club and SLBF ID |
| **All players** | Searchable list of every player with clubs and registration dates (also **/allPlayers**) |
| **Admin accounts** | Create another admin (date of birth and gender required, NIC optional), then **Create Admin Account** → **Confirm & Create** |

Buttons are disabled while a request is running, so double-clicking never submits twice.

### Bottom-right buttons
- **📜 Logs** — Activity logs: date & time, user (first and last name), role, description. Filter by **From / To** date & time, **User** (name, email or id) and **Role**. Newest first, 50 per page. Entries older than one month are removed automatically.
- **⚙️ Settings** — Home page **Main Title**, **Subtitle**, **Hero Banner Image** (recommended 1920×600, centred text) and the open club change period. Click **Save Changes**.

### Posts
**All Posts** → **New post**: title, optional subtitle, content, cover image (PNG/JPEG) → **Save Post**. Use **Edit** / **Delete** on a post card to change or remove it. Posts appear on the home page, newest first.

## Supported browsers
Tested on Chrome (Windows, Android), Microsoft Edge, Firefox and Safari's engine (macOS, iPhone). Phone screens are supported; the menu collapses into ☰.
